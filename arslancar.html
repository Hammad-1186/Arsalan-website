The legendary Toyota Land Cruiser is a globally celebrated luxury SUV known for its unparalleled off-road capability and rugged reliability. BMW

The BMW M5 is a high-performance, executive plug-in hybrid super sedan. Starting around $125,300 in the US or roughly 4.5 Crore PKR in Pakistan, it pairs a 4.4L TwinPower Turbo V8 with an electric motor to deliver a staggering 727 horsepower and 1,000 Nm of torque.
